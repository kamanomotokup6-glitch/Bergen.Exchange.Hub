  <div class="card">

    <h2>✈️ Day 1: Arrival & Transport</h2>

    <ul>

      <li><span class="tag">App</span><strong>Skyss Billett:</strong> Download this before landing to buy bus & Bybanen (light rail) tickets. Zone A covers the whole city & airport.</li>

      <li><span class="tag">Airport</span><strong>Flesland (BGO) to City / Fantoft:</strong> Take Bybanen Line 1 directly from the airport terminal.</li>

      <li><span class="tag">Housing</span><strong>Sammen Keys:</strong> Check your key pickup location in advance (often Nygårdsgaten or Fantoft service center).</li>

    </ul>

  </div>


  <div class="card">

    <h2>🛒 Groceries & Saving Money</h2>

    <ul>

      <li><span class="tag">Budget</span><strong>REMA 1000 & Kiwi:</strong> Generally the most student-friendly supermarkets. Download the <em>Æ</em> and <em>Trumf</em> apps for discounts.</li>

      <li><span class="tag">Food</span><strong>Too Good To Go:</strong> Rescue unsold bakery items and groceries at 1/3 of the original price.</li>

      <li><span class="tag">Rain</span><strong>Weather Gear:</strong> Umbrellas break in Bergen wind—invest in a proper waterproof rain jacket and waterproof shoes.</li>

    </ul>

  </div>


  <div class="card">

    <h2>🎓 Campus & Admin Essentials</h2>

    <ul>

      <li><span class="tag">Study</span><strong>Mitt UiB:</strong> Your main portal for course materials, schedules, and messages.</li>

      <li><span class="tag">Police</span><strong>UDI Registration:</strong> Book your appointment early if you are staying over 3 months (non-EU/EEA students).</li>

      <li><span class="tag">Health</span><strong>Legevakt (116 117):</strong> Urgent medical care clinic in Bergen when your GP isn't available.</li>

    </ul>

💳 Surviving Without Vipps & a Norwegian Bank

The #1 Exchange Trap: Because getting a D-number and Norwegian bank account takes months (or isn't eligible for 1-semester stays), most exchange students cannot get Vipps.
  • EssentialGet Wise or Revolut: Open a multi-currency account before arriving. Almost all international students in Bergen split bills via Revolut, Wise, or PayPal.
  • EventsStudent Org Tickets (ESN / Kvarteret): If a student event asks for Vipps payment, message the organizers on Instagram/Facebook—they almost always provide a card link or cash/Revolut option for exchange students.
  • CashDon't Withdraw NOK Cash: Norway is nearly 100% cashless. Even small bakeries and buses require contactless card or Apple/Google Pay.

🛒 Groceries, Cheap Eats & Bergen Rain

  • SupermarketsREMA 1000 & Kiwi: Cheapest everyday groceries. Look for store brands like First Price and Prima.
  • Food WasteToo Good To Go: Check the app around 20:00–22:00 for 39–49 NOK mystery bags from Godt Brød, Baker Brun, and Meny.
  • SecondhandFinn.no & Facebook Groups: Search "Exchange Students Bergen" on Facebook for cheap duvets, kitchenware, and bikes from departing students.
  • WeatherForget Umbrellas: Horizontal rain + wind destroys umbrellas in days. Buy a waterproof shell jacket (water column 10,000mm+) and waterproof boots.

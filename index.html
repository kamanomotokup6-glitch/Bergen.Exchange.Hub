🚀 Quick Survival Guide

  • No Vipps & Money: Use Revolut or Wise for bill splitting since standard Vipps requires a Norwegian D-number.
  • Skyss Public Transport: Download the Skyss Billett app to buy student-discounted tickets once your Studentbevis is active.
  • Sammen Housing & Utvask: Pay rent via the Sammen portal and remember to deep-clean thoroughly before moving out to avoid extra fees.

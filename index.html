UiB Exchange Survival Guide 🇳🇴

Real tips for international students in Bergen — by students, for students.

✈️ Day 1: Arrival & Transport

  • AppSkyss Billett: Download this before landing to buy bus & Bybanen (light rail) tickets. Zone A covers the whole city & airport.
  • AirportFlesland (BGO) to City / Fantoft: Take Bybanen Line 1 directly from the airport terminal.
  • HousingSammen Keys: Check your key pickup location in advance (often Nygårdsgaten or Fantoft service center).

🛒 Groceries & Saving Money

  • BudgetREMA 1000 & Kiwi: Generally the most student-friendly supermarkets. Download the Æ and Trumf apps for discounts.
  • FoodToo Good To Go: Rescue unsold bakery items and groceries at 1/3 of the original price.
  • RainWeather Gear: Umbrellas break in Bergen wind—invest in a proper waterproof rain jacket and waterproof shoes.

🎓 Campus & Admin Essentials

  • StudyMitt UiB: Your main portal for course materials, schedules, and messages.
  • PoliceUDI Registration: Book your appointment early if you are staying over 3 months (non-EU/EEA students).
  • HealthLegevakt (116 117): Urgent medical care clinic in Bergen when your GP isn't available.
